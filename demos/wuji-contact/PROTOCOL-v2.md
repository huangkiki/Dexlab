# Prospective input/controller correction within the original #251 allowance

The v1 campaign stopped after its first failed positive control. It is not rerun or relabeled: the raw 8,800-step record, original scorer report and source commit `461a986e0dc8ed994ae75cbe93ff36a52ef07830` remain immutable. No v1 negative controls were executed. The new v2 cases below have different declared inputs/controller, so they cannot isolate a solver algorithm effect or overwrite that denominator.

Static replay identified **4.9808 mm initial wrist/thumb-abduction self-penetration**, before grasping began, plus wrist/ring and wrist/pinky overlap. The convex wrist filled the source recesses. The object-only static check missed this; the actual entry now checks every native initial contact and rejects penetration above the original 1 mm tolerance before `mj_step`. A separate unconstrained linearization of the explicit sampled PD has spectral radius about 279 at that pose. This is a competing controller problem, not proof that the geometry alone caused the entire trajectory.

Two concrete corrections are prospectively frozen in `protocol-v2.json`:

1. Use MuJoCo's native SDF generated from the **same wrist source mesh**, octree depth 9, 40 initial points and 100 SDF iterations. Other link collision meshes and the analytic sphere remain unchanged. Do not disable wrist/finger collision or add exclusions. Static native reconstruction has zero initial contacts; that is input admission, not dynamic success or exact SDF equivalence to another engine.
2. Evaluate the same PD gains at the predicted end of the actual 2 ms zero-order hold. With native step `h`, hold `H=4h`, and `c=H(H+h)/2`, solve `(M+cK+HD)a = K(q_target-q-Hv)-Dv`, then request `Ma+bias` and retain the same native effort caps. This follows the endpoint PD law under constant-acceleration semi-implicit microsteps; contacts, saturation and varying inertia are excluded from that prediction and remain measured in native dynamics. Independent linear integration tests check the equation. The initial unconstrained spectral radius is about 0.751; this does not establish nonlinear contact stability.

Keep the original sphere, gravity, friction, initial target, control/physics clocks, gains, times, six case order and **every acceptance threshold unchanged**. Also correct a reporting error: falling clear of the hand without a qualified preceding hold is not successful active release. The old `release_pass=true` was only a drop/no-contact predicate; v2 reports that predicate separately and requires qualified holding for active-release success. Both original and corrected offline reports must be preserved; no physics rerun is required for that stricter interpretation.

The v2 ledger uses distinct `v2-` case IDs, inherits v1's one start and measured cost, and retains the original work-package ceiling of **12 starts / 7,200 seconds total**. This is not a new allowance. Source/protocol hashes and the measured resource plan are frozen before any v2 physics. At most six new v2 cases, in the order from the original protocol; stop this corrected campaign on invalid records or a failed positive. Further outcomes may require a new maintainer decision rather than another automatic redesign. No tuning sweep, new engine, threshold relaxation, latch or external scored support is introduced.

All other terms, raw epochs, force/momentum checks, failure preservation and the historical 74-row applicability matrix remain as in [v1](PROTOCOL.md). Neither campaign is #48's ten-condition post-development validation.
