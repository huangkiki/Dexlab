# Research guide

Mode: Read. The source is the self-contained `_static/research/dexterous-simulation.html`; its article content, evidence boundaries, filters and external links stay unchanged.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Turn a long mixed research log into four discernible reading lanes without hiding the underlying evidence. Readers should understand what is foundational, what is an evidence index, what is a dated update, and what belongs to systems/policy.

OWN-WORLD: Keep the warm-paper article, serif opening, restrained green links and fine rules. Use a grouped reading rail, a two-column guide map and open editorial rows; no cards, gradients or dashboard chrome.

STORY: Establish screening and the six problem layers, inspect seven key trade-offs, then choose one of three starting experiments. Use the evidence index for papers, repositories and the full report. Treat 2026 observations and systems/policies as separate research lanes instead of appending numbered chapters.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Global masthead; grouped local rail on desktop; date, full research question, scope, evidence loop and summary counts. The four-lane guide map follows immediately after the counts.

ADAPTATION: The rail becomes a four-column grouped map below the masthead at (1000px), then a two-column map at (600px). Two-column evidence and layer grids and the three-column experiment route stack at (760px). Table wrappers retain their own horizontal scrolling.

MOTION: No new decorative motion. Existing smooth anchor scrolling respects reduced motion. The current rail destination is indicated with `aria-current="location"`.
