# Research guide

Mode: Read. The source is the self-contained `_static/research/dexterous-simulation.html`; its article content, evidence boundaries, filters and external links stay unchanged.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Turn a long mixed research log into two foundational lanes plus four persistent topics without hiding the underlying evidence. Readers should understand what is foundational, what is an evidence index, and which task, physics, data or evaluation topic they want next.

OWN-WORLD: Keep the warm-paper article, serif opening, restrained green links and fine rules. Use a grouped reading rail, a two-column guide map and open editorial rows; no cards, gradients or dashboard chrome.

STORY: Establish screening and the six problem layers, inspect seven key trade-offs, then choose one of three starting experiments. Use the evidence index for papers, repositories and the full report. New papers and systems are distributed into task/execution, physics/perception, data/skills and evaluation/real-gains topics; dates remain supporting metadata rather than the taxonomy.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Global masthead; grouped local rail on desktop; date, full research question, scope, evidence loop and summary counts. The four-lane guide map follows immediately after the counts.

ADAPTATION: The rail becomes a three-column grouped map below the masthead at (1000px). At (600px) it collapses to a native “阅读目录” disclosure, and its expanded groups use two columns. Two-column evidence and layer grids and the three-column experiment route stack at (760px). Table wrappers retain their own horizontal scrolling.

MOTION: No new decorative motion. Existing smooth anchor scrolling respects reduced motion. The current rail destination is indicated with `aria-current="location"`.
