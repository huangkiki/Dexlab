# Engine comparison experiments

Mode: Read. Scope: `zh/visual-comparisons.md`, `en/visual-comparisons.md`, their replay UI and comparison tables.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A reader should understand the decision before inspecting the medium. Each experiment states its conclusion, criteria and scope, then shows synchronized visual evidence and a compact matrix that explains what passed, failed or remained invalid.

OWN-WORLD: Warm-paper research ledger with fine rules and recorded experiment colors. No card grid, ranking dashboard or promotional treatment.

STORY: What question was tested, under which shared conditions, what happened in each configuration, which numerical criterion decided it, and what remains outside the conclusion.

FIRST VIEWPORT FOR EACH EXPERIMENT: A verdict with criteria and scope, followed by a short replay guide and the recorded comparison. The long protocol comes after the visible evidence.

VISUAL EVIDENCE: The recorder adds pane labels from the recorded series list above each video. A three-part guide names pane order, what to watch and what cannot be inferred from the image. Playback remains muted and user-started.

RESULT MATRIX: Counts and explicit result words replace a single headline percentage. Pass, fail, partial and invalid states use both text and color. Invalid records remain in the denominator and are never rewritten as physical failures. The legend states that fixed configurations are not a universal ranking.

ADAPTATION: Summary and guide columns stack below 760px. Matrices retain an internal horizontal scroll at a 760px minimum width. Replay panes stack at 560px.
