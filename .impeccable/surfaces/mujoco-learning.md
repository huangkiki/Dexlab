# MuJoCo learning route

Mode: Read, with small Operate interactions. Targets: docs/site/learning interface and mujoco-learning lessons in both languages.
Audience: robotics readers moving from findings to an understanding of stepping, contact and performance. The first learning route prioritizes MuJoCo, connecting concepts to integration questions relevant to MATRiX without asserting a runtime version or new measurements.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A continuous, question-led course; chapter navigation and one worked example replace a directory of repositories. Adopt the interaction logic of the user-pinned RL × Robotics learning route.

OWN-WORLD: Extend DexLab's established warm paper, charcoal, muted green and restrained rules. Native reading controls, serif lesson headings, sans body and navigation. No new visual identity.

STORY: Read one intuition, change a quantity, explain the result, then follow the next lesson or the existing deeper course. Progress is an explicit reading bookmark, never a competence score.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim masthead; a persistent chapter rail on the left, a readable central lesson, a quiet section index on wide screens. Mobile puts a collapsible chapter list before the article. The first lesson names the practical question; the step lesson places the adjustable timing example immediately after its explanation.

FORM: Code-led extension, pinned reference; no concept roll or approved image comp. Source-backed lessons link to preserved 3.15 material and separately dated 4.0 release changes. No runtime or performance claims from browser diagrams.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
